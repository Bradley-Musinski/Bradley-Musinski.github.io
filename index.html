About Me

Who I Am as a Developer

My passion for game development began with my love for video games, especially the Resident Evil franchise, which inspired me through its immersive atmosphere, storytelling, and gameplay. After serving in the U.S. Army, I decided to pursue game development at Full Sail University. I enjoy solving programming challenges, designing gameplay systems, exploring new systems and ideas, and bringing those ideas to life. Through my studies and personal projects, I've strengthened my skills in C++, object-oriented programming, and Unreal Engine. My goal is to become a gameplay programmer and create memorable experiences that inspire others just as Resident Evil inspired me.
